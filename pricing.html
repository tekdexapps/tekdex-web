Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

1. Our approach

This is a static marketing website. It does not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, it does not build a profile of you, and it does not sell or share personal data with advertisers.

2. What we collect

We only receive personal information that you choose to send us:

  • Enquiries. The name, email address, service of interest and message you enter in a contact form.
  • Applications. The name, email address, current company and CV you send when applying for a role.
  • Correspondence. Anything you include when you email us directly.

The forms on this site do not post to a server. When you submit one, your browser opens your own email application with the details filled in — nothing reaches us until you press send there, and you can edit or abandon the message first.

3. Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, to assess your application, and to keep a record of our correspondence with you. We do not use your details for marketing unless you ask us to.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to deal with them and for a reasonable period afterwards. Applications are kept for the duration of the hiring process, and for up to twelve months afterwards in case a suitable role opens — unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

5. Who else sees it

Your information is handled by the TekDex people who need it to reply to you or assess your application. We use standard business email and document services to do that, and we may disclose information where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

6. Cookies

We do not set analytics, advertising or profiling cookies. Your browser may store limited technical data locally to make the site work; that data stays on your device.

7. Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to consultations@tekdexinc.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send us confidential material, credentials or identification documents through this site.

If we change this policy, the updated version will appear here with a new date.
