ServiceNow Platform

ServiceNow is turning into an AI platform, and Now Assist is the reason. Case summarisation, generated resolution notes, catalogue and flow generation, and AI Search that actually answers — the work is shifting from writing scripts to designing the guardrails those models operate inside. That is where we spend most of our time now.

Around that we work the full platform — ITSM and ITOM for service and operations, ITAM with SAM Pro and HAM Pro for asset and licence position, HRSD and CSM for employee and customer journeys, SecOps and GRC for risk, and SPM for the portfolio view. Discovery, Service Mapping and a CMDB you can trust sit underneath, because Now Assist and Predictive Intelligence are only as good as the data they read.
